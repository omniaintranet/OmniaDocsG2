Content flow AI prompts
==========================

Available in Omnia 7.12 and later. Note: Content flow is also called Content builder.

Here you find a number of suggested prompts to use in the Content flow (Content builder) block. You can edit them to your liking.

.. image:: content-flow-ai.png 

All AI prompts are not shown in the image.

To edit a prompt, just open it and do the changes.

.. image:: content-flow-ai-edit.png 

Save when you're done.

There's also an option to reset all prompts to default, if nedded.
